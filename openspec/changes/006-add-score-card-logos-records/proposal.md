# Change: Add team logos and records to Scores cards

## Why
Scores cards show team names and scores but lack quick visual cues and context. Logos and records improve scanability and context at a glance.

## What Changes
- Display team logos for home and away in score cards.
- Show each team record (W-L-OT) from standings data when available.

## Impact
- Affected specs: scores-view (modified)
- Affected code: resources/js/Pages/LabScores.tsx, resources/js/lib/nhl.ts
